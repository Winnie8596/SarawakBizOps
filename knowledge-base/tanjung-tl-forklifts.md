# Tanjung TL-series Forklifts — Workshop Manual

Covers the Tanjung TL-35D (3.5 t, diesel) and TL-25E (2.5 t, electric). All content is synthetic and written for the
SarawakBizOps demonstration. Sections for site technicians are given in Bahasa Malaysia as well as English.

## Safety

Park on level ground, lower the forks fully, apply the parking brake and remove the key. On the TL-25E, also disconnect
the battery connector before working on any electrical part. Never work under raised forks or a raised mast without
the mast safety chain fitted.

## Keselamatan (Bahasa Malaysia)

Letak forklift di atas tanah rata, turunkan garpu sepenuhnya, tarik brek tangan dan cabut kunci. Untuk TL-25E,
cabut penyambung bateri sebelum kerja elektrik. Jangan bekerja di bawah garpu atau mast yang diangkat tanpa rantai
keselamatan mast dipasang.

## Engine will not start (TL-35D)

If the starter clicks but does not turn on cold mornings, check the battery first: clean corroded battery terminals,
protect them with terminal grease and load-test the battery. If the battery is good, the starter solenoid contacts
are likely worn; replace the contact set.

## Enjin tidak hidup (TL-35D)

Jika starter berbunyi "klik" tetapi enjin tidak berpusing pada waktu pagi yang sejuk, periksa bateri dahulu:
bersihkan terminal bateri yang berkarat, sapu gris terminal dan uji beban bateri. Jika bateri baik, sesentuh solenoid
starter mungkin haus; tukar set sesentuh.

## Hydraulic lift cylinder leaks

Oil running down the lift cylinder, or forks that drift down with a load, mean the lift cylinder seals are worn.
Lower the forks fully and relieve hydraulic pressure by operating the lift lever with the engine off before opening
any hydraulic line. Replace the seals with the lift-cylinder seal kit (part SEAL-KIT-HYD) and refill with ISO VG 46
hydraulic oil (part OIL-HYD-46).

## Kebocoran silinder angkat hidraulik

Minyak mengalir di silinder angkat, atau garpu turun sendiri semasa membawa beban, bermaksud seal silinder sudah haus.
Turunkan garpu dan lepaskan tekanan hidraulik dengan menggerakkan tuil angkat semasa enjin mati sebelum membuka
mana-mana paip hidraulik. Tukar seal menggunakan kit seal silinder (SEAL-KIT-HYD) dan isi minyak hidraulik ISO VG 46
(OIL-HYD-46).

## Fault codes

### E14 Hydraulic oil temperature high

**Meaning on TL-series forklifts:** hydraulic oil above 95 °C. Stop work, let the oil cool and check the oil level and
the oil cooler fins.
