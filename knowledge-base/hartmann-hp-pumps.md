# Hartmann HP-series Centrifugal Pumps — Service Notes

Covers the Hartmann HP-150, HP-100 and HP-80 end-suction centrifugal pumps. All content is synthetic and written for
the SarawakBizOps demonstration.

## Safety

Isolate and lock off the motor before touching the coupling or the pump. Close the suction and discharge valves and
drain the casing before opening it: the casing may hold liquid under pressure and at process temperature.

## Bearing housing running hot

A hot, noisy bearing housing usually means the grease has broken down from over-temperature or the coupling is
misaligned. Flush and re-grease the bearings with the specified grease, laser-align the coupling and monitor housing
temperature for an hour; it should settle below 70 °C.

## Low flow and low discharge pressure

If flow drops and the discharge gauge reads low, check for a blocked impeller eye (fibre or debris from the process)
and measure the wear ring clearance. Replace the wear ring when clearance is above 0.5 mm. Confirm flow and discharge
pressure against the pump curve after the repair.

## Mechanical seal leaking

A steady drip from the seal area means the mechanical seal faces are worn. Replace the seal (part SEAL-MECH-35 on
HP-150). Never run the pump dry: a mechanical seal fails within seconds without liquid.
